Offshoot Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 5, 2026

Offshoot is built to keep your thoughts private. The short version: your data stays on your device, and we never see it.

What we collect

Nothing. Offshoot does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-ins, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

Where your data lives

Everything you create in Offshoot, including your thoughts, branches, and notes, is stored only on your device. That includes content you add through Offshoot’s widget, share extension, or Siri and Shortcuts features. None of it is sent to us or to any third party.

If you choose to export a backup or share something from Offshoot, it goes only where you send it.

Device backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up your device to a computer, your Offshoot data may be included in those backups. Apple’s privacy policy covers those backups, and we have no access to them.

Deleting your data

You’re in control. You can delete individual items in the app at any time. Deleting the Offshoot app removes all of its data from your device.

Children’s privacy

Offshoot does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If Offshoot ever changes how it handles data, we’ll update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.

Contact
