Questions, problems or ideas.

Something not working, or have an idea? Email offshoot.support@gmail.com. It helps to include your iPhone model and iOS version.

How do I delete my data?

Offshoot keeps everything on your device, so deleting the app deletes all of its data: touch and hold the Offshoot icon, tap Remove App, then Delete App. To remove single thoughts or Seeds instead, delete them inside the app.

If you want to keep a copy first, go to You → Export everything before you delete the app.

For how Offshoot handles your information, read the privacy policy.
